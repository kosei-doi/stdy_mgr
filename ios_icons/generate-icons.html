📱 iOS App Icon Generator

This tool generates all the required icon sizes for iPhone/iPad apps from your SVG icon.

📋 iOS Icon Requirements

The following icon sizes will be generated:

  • 40x40px - iPhone Notification (iOS 7-11)
  • 60x60px - iPhone App (iOS 7+)
  • 76x76px - iPad App (iOS 7+)
  • 83.5x83.5px - iPad Pro App (iOS 9+)
  • 1024x1024px - App Store
